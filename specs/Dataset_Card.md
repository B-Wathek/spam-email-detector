# Dataset Card — Spam Email Dataset

## 1. Dataset Name
Spam Email Detection Dataset

---

## 2. Purpose
Used for training and evaluating a binary spam classifier.

---

## 3. Composition
- 10 sample rows (HW version)
- Balanced spam / not_spam labels

---

## 4. Source
- Synthetic + manually created samples

---

## 5. Labeling
- 0 = not spam
- 1 = spam
- Manually annotated

---

## 6. Non-Use Cases
- Not for production training
- Not suitable for legal or financial classification
- Not representative of real-world scale

---

## 7. Known Limitations
- Small dataset size
- No multilingual support
- No real-world noise

---

## 8. Version
v1.0 (Homework dataset)
